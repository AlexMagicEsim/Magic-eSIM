How it works

  1. Pick your destination and a data plan.
  2. Check your phone supports eSIM and is not carrier-locked.
  3. Install the eSIM profile before you fly.
  4. Land, switch it on, and you are online.
Will my phone work?

Your phone needs eSIM support and must not be carrier-locked. Most phones released after 2019 qualify.
