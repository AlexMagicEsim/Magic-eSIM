eSIM Installation & Activation

Your device must support eSIM and must not be locked to a mobile operator. Please check compatibility before you buy; if you are unsure, contact us first.

Our guides explain each step:

  • How to install an eSIM on iPhone
  • How to install an eSIM on Android
  • Does your phone support eSIM?
  • When to install and turn on your eSIM
  • eSIM not working — what to check
  • You need an internet connection, for example Wi-Fi, to install the eSIM.
  • An eSIM can normally be installed only once and only on one device. It cannot be moved to another device.
  • Do not delete an installed eSIM. A deleted eSIM usually cannot be restored.
  • When the validity period starts is set for each plan. Depending on the plan, it is the installation of the eSIM, its first connection to a network, its first use of data, or the purchase.
  • An eSIM that is not activated within its plan’s activation period expires unused. If you do not intend to install the eSIM soon after buying it, ask us for the period of your plan before you pay.

Connectivity & Roaming Issues

  • Data roaming usually has to be switched on for the Magic eSIM line. Switch it on for the Magic eSIM line only.
  • To avoid charges from your home mobile operator, keep data roaming switched off for your other SIM and choose the Magic eSIM line for mobile data.
  • Plans connect to partner networks in the destination as a roaming connection, and your connection may be routed through another country. Websites and apps may therefore see an IP address or location outside the country you are in.
  • Coverage, speed and availability depend on the local operator’s network, your location, your device and its settings, and network load.
  • Plans provide mobile data. They do not include a phone number, voice calls or SMS unless the product card says so.
  • In some destinations the law or the network operator requires users of certain plans to register their identity. The plan may not work until registration is complete.
  • Some countries require a device brought from abroad to be registered after a period of stay. An unregistered device may be blocked by the local networks, whichever eSIM it uses.

If your plan does not connect, start with the checks in eSIM not working — what to check. If it still does not work, write to us. Please do not delete the eSIM while we are helping you. We try to fix the problem, for example by guiding you through settings, reissuing installation details or providing a replacement eSIM.

Refunds

Our Refund Policy is part of our Terms of Service. It applies to plans bought once international checkout becomes available.

Paid purchases are not refundable or exchangeable except as required by the law that applies to your purchase or as expressly provided in the Refund Policy:

  • A duplicate payment, or any amount charged in error, is refunded in full.
  • If we could not deliver the eSIM you paid for, or we cancel your order, we refund the full amount paid.
  • For a technical fault that our support has confirmed and cannot fix, we offer a replacement eSIM or a full or partial refund.
  • Your statutory rights, including the right of withdrawal where the law gives it, are always honoured.

For any other reason — you chose the wrong plan, your device is not compatible, your trip changed, you did not use the eSIM — there is no voluntary refund. The Refund Policy sets out the details.

Information Needed for Support

To help us find your order and look into the problem, please include in your message:

  • your order reference;
  • the email address used for the order;
  • your device model and its operating system version;
  • the destination where you use, or plan to use, the eSIM;
  • a short description of the problem and, if possible, screenshots of the error.

Never send us a card number or security code, a password, or the eSIM’s QR code or installation details. We never receive or store your full card number or security code. The QR code and installation details give access to your plan. Do not share them.
