# On-screen labels — Spanish (es)

here must not be quoted (describe the action in words). Where Spain (es-ES) and Mexico/Latin America (es-MX, es-419)
differ, a neutral guide gives both, Spain first. Source pages are the same ones `seo/guides-es.mjs` names.
