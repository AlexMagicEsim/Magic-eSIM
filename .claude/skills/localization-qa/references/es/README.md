# Spanish (es) — what «right» means on this site

- **Variant:** neutral international Spanish for a Latin-American-first audience (owner decision D1, 2026-10-21). Address
  the reader as **tú**; never «vosotros»; no «usted». Avoid Spain-only words (ordenador, coger, pinchar, vale, móvil as
  the phone) and Latin-American slang; prefer what both understand (teléfono, computadora, elegir, tocar).
- **Decided, not findings:** prices «USD 12.99» (es-419); English slugs in URLs; «eSIM» feminine («la/una eSIM»);
  the slogan «Stay Connected Anywhere» in English; both regional labels side by side in the guides
  («Ajustes → Datos móviles (Configuración → Datos celulares)»).
- **Punctuation:** opening ¿ and ¡; «comillas latinas» are the site's quotes; no space before : ; ? !; a colon before an
  explanation is fine; sentence case in headings (no Title Case).
- **Numbers:** decimal point in data amounts («1.5 GB»), matching the es-419 prices.
- **Plural and gender:** «1 día / 2 días», «1 país / 2 países»; «y» → «e» before an /i/ sound («Francia e Italia»).
- **SEO:** titles ≤ 60 characters where possible, descriptions ≤ 160; the title states the intent («eSIM para X»).
- **Claims:** the English is the legal reference. «ilimitado», «rápido», «garantizado», «te enviaremos…», payment methods
  — never, unless the English says the same.
