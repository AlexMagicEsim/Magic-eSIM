# Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans) — what «right» means on this site

- **Variant:** neutral Simplified Chinese (owner D3, 2026-10-22), PRC punctuation norms, Apple zh-cn terms. The reader is
  addressed with **您** (owner D1) — never 你 in the site's own copy. Readers are anyone who reads Simplified Chinese
  (mainland China, Singapore, Malaysia, the diaspora), not only mainland China.
- **Decided, not findings:** prices **«US$12.99»** (Intl zh-Hans, currency symbol — owner D2); English slugs in URLs (D4);
  data in **GB / MB**; the slogan «Stay Connected Anywhere» in English; country names = CLDR with the owner's overrides
  (D5): 中国香港, 中国澳门, 台湾 (CLDR), 阿联酋 (not 阿拉伯联合酋长国), 埃斯瓦蒂尼 (not 斯威士兰); the destination list
  grouped by pinyin initial; **no legal links in the footer** until the English LEGAL documents are published (D8 — the
  Chinese pages never point at the Russian documents; «条款/隐私» words exist in the dictionary only for that day).
- **Mainland China (D12):** the compatibility guide quotes Apple 123879 (zh-cn, 2026-09-09): the iPhone models bought in
  mainland China that support eSIM, and «在中国大陆，无法安装境外运营商提供的 eSIM» → «请在入境前安装 eSIM». This is decided
  and sourced; the iPhone guide and the home's compatibility note point to it («在中国大陆购买的手机，只有部分机型支持 eSIM»)
  — deliberately stronger than the English «most phones released after 2019 qualify», which is not true there. Never add
  any statement about which services (Google, WhatsApp, VPN…) work in mainland China.
- **Typography (D6):** full-width punctuation ，。：；？！、（） and “ ” quotes in Chinese text; a half-width space between
  Chinese and Latin letters or digits («安装 eSIM», «等待 1 到 3 分钟», «iPhone 18 Pro»), none next to full-width
  punctuation; the em dash ——; an ellipsis …. Latin product names, units and codes stay as they are.
- **Numbers:** decimal point («1.5 GB»), thousands with a comma («US$1,234.50»); «{n} 天», «{n} 个国家/地区».
- **Plural:** Chinese has one form; `.one` and `.other` carry the same text — not a finding.
- **SEO:** budgets are DISPLAY WIDTH (a Chinese character = 2 units): titles ≤ 60 units, descriptions ≤ 160 units.
- **Claims:** the English is the legal reference (plus D12). 无限, 快速, 高速, 保证, 24 小时, 最佳/最便宜/第一 (PRC Advertising
  Law art. 9), 退款, payment methods (支付宝, 微信支付), VPN / 翻墙 / access to Google — never (seo/claim-guards.mjs zh-Hans).
- **Samsung:** no Samsung page in Simplified Chinese naming the eSIM menus was found (D13): Samsung steps are described,
  never quoted. Quoting a Samsung label is a finding.
