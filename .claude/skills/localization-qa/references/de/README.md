# German (de) — what «right» means on this site

- **Variant:** standard German (de-DE), understood in Germany, Austria and Switzerland (owner D3, 2026-10-22). No
  dialect, no Helvetisms or Austriacisms; «ß» as in de-DE. Address the reader as **du** (owner D1) — as Apple's,
  Google's and Samsung's own German support pages do; never «Sie» in the site's copy (a vendor page quoting «Sie» is not
  our copy). Lower-case «du/dein» (current rules; capitalised «Du» only in letters) — a reviewer's point, not a check: `check.mjs` matches case-insensitively.
- **Decided, not findings:** prices «12,99 USD» (de-DE, the currency as a code — owner D2); English slugs in URLs (D4);
  «die eSIM» (feminine, as «die SIM»); the slogan «Stay Connected Anywhere» in English; legal links «AGB / Datenschutz
  (auf Russisch)» (D8); a country name after «für» carries the article German needs, in the accusative («für die
  Türkei», «für die Vereinigten Staaten», «für den Irak») from `DE_FUER` in seo/page-copy.mjs — a template never puts a
  name in the dative.
- **Punctuation:** „…“ are German quotes (the guides quote on-screen labels in bold, not in quotes); a spaced en dash
  « – » as the dash; a space before «…» when words are left out («Tarife werden geladen …»); no space before : ; ? !;
  sentence case in headings (nouns capitalised, as German always does — that is not Title Case).
- **Numbers:** decimal comma («1,5 GB»), thousands with a point in prices («1.234,50 USD»); units kbit/s, Mbit/s.
- **Plural:** «1 Tag / 2 Tage», «1 Land / 2 Länder».
- **SEO:** titles ≤ 60 characters, descriptions ≤ 160; a country title states the intent («eSIM für X»).
- **Claims:** the English is the legal reference. «unbegrenzt», «Flatrate», «schnell», «garantiert», «rund um die Uhr»,
  «wir schicken dir …», payment methods — never, unless the English says the same (seo/claim-guards.mjs, de).
